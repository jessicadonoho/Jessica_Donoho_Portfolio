a little about me

From classrooms in Okinawa to UX research

Before grad school I spent two years in Okinawa as an Assistant Language Teacher with the JET Program. Running a classroom taught me to pay close attention to people: noticing where they get stuck and changing plans on the fly.

Now I'm a Master's student at the University of Michigan School of Information, focusing on UX research and User-Centered Agile Development. I'm drawn to mission-driven work, where good research can make a real difference in people's day.

Away from the laptop I'm writing, doing fiber arts, reading manga, or out with my camera. I picked up photography while exploring villages in Japan, and that's where this site's look comes from.
